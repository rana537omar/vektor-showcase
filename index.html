How it works for a client

Four steps, and only the first three need the client.

1

Sign in

Every client has a private account with its own balance, goals and reports.

2

Pick a risk style

Conservative, balanced or aggressive. One button starts hands-off trading.

3

Set goals

Grow to a value by a date, a monthly profit, a hard loss limit, a win rate.

4

Read the reports

The system trades on its own and writes a report every day and every week.

Paper trading: everything runs on a simulated account with live market prices. No trading system can guarantee profit, and nothing here is financial advice.

Everything behind the one button

An autonomous trader, the tools to check its work, and the reporting clients expect.

Goals that act

  • Loss limit as a hard floor: breach it and all automated trading stops and positions are sold.
  • Lock in a reached goal by stopping trading the moment it is hit.
  • Progress checked every minute, with a notification on every change.
  • No chasing: risk is never raised to catch up.

Automatic reports

  • Daily and weekly, written without anyone asking.
  • Plain language first, then the numbers, goal status and every trade.
  • Print or save as PDF, or generate one on demand.

AI Autopilot

  • Scans every market on every new candle and backtests six strategies on each.
  • Trades only what passes its checks on both older and recent data.
  • Risk-sized positions with automatic stops and a daily loss limit.
  • Stays in cash when nothing qualifies, and logs every decision.

AI signals

  • A model trained on your own machine for each market, with no outside AI service.
  • Probability, confidence and drivers for every signal.
  • Honest accuracy, scored on history the model never saw.

Trading terminal

  • Live prices and order book.
  • Charts with moving averages, Bollinger bands, RSI and MACD.
  • Orders with stop loss, take profit and trailing stops.
  • Price alerts and a notification center.

Research and control

  • Backtester with fees and slippage, against buy and hold.
  • Optimizer that flags the robust parameter sets.
  • Strategy bots and detailed analytics with CSV export.
  • Private accounts with hashed passwords.
